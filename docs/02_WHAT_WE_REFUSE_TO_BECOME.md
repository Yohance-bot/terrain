# What We Refuse To Become

**Status:** Approved

Split out of `00_PROJECT_VISION` without change of meaning, because it is referenced as a document in its own right.

As this project grows, there will always be pressure to increase engagement, monetize more aggressively, add trending features, or optimize purely for growth.

This document exists to define the principles we will never compromise.

These are not technical decisions.

They are product decisions.

They define the character of the project.

---

## We Will Never Replace Movement With Money

Movement is the only true source of progress.

Players may one day purchase cosmetic items, convenience features, or optional content, but spending money must never substitute for physical movement.

No player should ever be able to purchase a stronger territory, conquer a city, or outperform another player simply by paying.

Victory must always be earned outside.

Not inside a checkout screen.

---

## We Will Never Encourage Unsafe Behaviour

The game must never ask players to put themselves at risk.

No feature should encourage players to:

- cross dangerous roads
- ignore traffic
- enter private property
- exercise in unsafe environments
- chase notifications while moving
- compete while distracted

The safest decision should always be the correct decision.

The real world always has priority over the game.

---

## VERY IMPORTANT: We Will Never Reward Fake Movement

A world that can be manipulated is a world that eventually loses meaning.

Every meaningful achievement within the game should represent genuine physical effort.

Cheating is more than an unfair advantage.

It damages the shared world that every honest player contributes to.

Protecting the integrity of movement is one of the most important responsibilities of the platform.

If players stop trusting the map, they stop caring about the map.

---

## We Will Never Let Competition Become Harassment

Competition should create stories.

It should never create fear.

Players should feel proud when someone challenges them.

They should never feel threatened.

The platform should actively discourage harassment, stalking, bullying, intimidation, or unwanted contact.

Owning the same territory should create healthy rivalry.

It should never create hostility between people.

Respect always comes before victory.

---

## We Will Never Punish People For Living Their Lives

Players should never feel guilty for taking a vacation.

Missing a day.

Getting injured.

Working late.

Spending time with family.

Or simply resting.

The game should encourage consistency without creating unhealthy obligation.

Healthy habits last for years.

Burnout lasts much longer.

---

## We Will Never Build Around Addiction

Many modern applications optimize for attention.

This project optimizes for action.

The goal is not to maximize screen time.

The goal is to maximize meaningful movement.

Players should spend more time outside than inside the application.

If a feature increases engagement but reduces real-world activity, it should be questioned.

---

## We Will Never Let Advertisements Break Immersion

Advertising should never interrupt the experience.

No pop-ups.

No full-screen advertisements.

No videos before gameplay.

No banners covering the map.

If businesses are ever introduced into the world, they should exist naturally within it.

A local café.

A healthy food stall.

A water refill station.

A sports store.

A marathon sponsor.

Advertisements should feel like places that belong in the city rather than interruptions that break immersion.

The world should remain believable.

---

## We Will Never Lose The Human Side

Behind every territory is a real person.

Behind every run is someone's effort.

Behind every achievement is time that cannot be replaced.

The platform should celebrate people.

Not just statistics.

Not just rankings.

Not just efficiency.

Communities should always matter more than leaderboards.

Friendships should outlast rivalries.

---

## We Will Never Treat Players As Products

The trust players place in the platform is earned.

Movement history, routes, achievements and activity may be collected to provide meaningful gameplay, progress tracking, social features and personal insights.

This information exists to improve the player's experience.

It should never exist simply because it can be collected.

The platform should always be transparent about what is stored, why it is stored, and how it improves the experience.

Trust is far harder to earn than to lose.

---

## We Will Never Forget Why We Started

This project was never created to become another fitness tracker.

It was never created to maximize advertising revenue.

It exists for one simple reason.

To make people excited to move.

Every feature, every line of code and every future decision should continue serving that purpose.

If we ever build something that moves us further away from that mission, we should have the courage to remove it.

---

## Permitted Monetization

Stated positively, so that it is decided in calm rather than under pressure later.

The following are acceptable:

- Cosmetic items
- Event participation and event partnerships
- Optional club features
- Sponsorship by local businesses, expressed as real places within the world

The following are not, and never will be:

- Any purchase that increases influence, territory, or competitive standing
- Interstitial, video, banner or pop-up advertising
- Sale of player movement data
